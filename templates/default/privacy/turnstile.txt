Protection of forms against automated abuse (Cloudflare Turnstile)

This website uses Cloudflare Turnstile on its forms to tell human visitors from automated programs, and so to protect the website and its users against spam and abuse. Turnstile is provided by Cloudflare, Inc. [country of the vendor, to be completed from the vendor's policy].

When a visitor reaches a page with a protected form, Turnstile processes client-side signals such as the client IP address, the TLS fingerprint, the User-Agent header and the sitekey with its associated origin, and sends them to Cloudflare.
