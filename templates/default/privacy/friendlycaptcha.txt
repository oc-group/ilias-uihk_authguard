Protection of forms against automated abuse (Friendly Captcha)

This website uses Friendly Captcha on its forms to tell human visitors from automated programs, and so to protect the website and its users against spam and abuse. Friendly Captcha is provided by Friendly Captcha GmbH, Germany.

When a visitor reaches a page with a protected form, the visitor's browser solves a small puzzle in the background and transfers connection data, environment data, interaction data and functional data to Friendly Captcha. The data are used only to identify and handle potential bots and risks. Friendly Captcha states that IP addresses are anonymised with a one-way hash function before they are stored, and that it sets no HTTP cookies.
