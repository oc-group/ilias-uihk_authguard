Protection of forms against automated abuse (hCaptcha)

This website uses the hCaptcha security service on its forms to check that a submission is made by a person and not by an automated program, and so to protect the website and its users against spam and abuse. hCaptcha is provided by Intuition Machines, Inc., a company incorporated in Delaware, USA ("hCaptcha").

When a visitor reaches a page with a protected form, hCaptcha analyses the visitor's behaviour automatically, in some cases in the background without showing any challenge. It processes data such as the IP address, browser type and device type, the time spent on the page and human-verification data such as mouse movements, scroll position, keypress events and touch events. The data are sent to hCaptcha.
